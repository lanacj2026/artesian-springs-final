About Artesian Springs

Local water.
A family behind every bottle.

We are a local, family-owned business built on something simple: clean, natural water sourced straight from our well in Grady County, OK.

For us, this isn’t just a product. It’s personal. Our water comes from the same source we trust for our own families. We believe you should know where your water comes from and who stands behind it.

Being family-owned means we pay attention to the details. From sourcing to bottling, we focus on quality, consistency, and doing things the right way — not the fastest way.

When you choose us, you’re supporting a local business, a real family, and water that comes from the ground, not a factory.

Deep Source, Pure Taste.
Why Artesian Springs?

What matters to us, from source to sip.

The same three ideas you see on our home page are the principles behind how we operate every day.

Well-Sourced

Our water comes directly from our own well in Grady County, Oklahoma. That connection to the source gives us something increasingly rare: a clear answer when someone asks where their water comes from.

Family Owned

Artesian Springs is run by a real local family, not a distant corporate brand. We care about every partnership, every order, and the quality of the product because our name and our community are attached to it.

Exceptionally Pure

We keep the focus where it belongs: clean, naturally sourced water, consistent quality, and a straightforward product we feel good about serving to our own families and yours.
